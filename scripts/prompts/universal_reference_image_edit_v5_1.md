# UNIVERSAL REFERENCE IMAGE EDIT V5.1

## [TASK]

Create ONE seamless, photorealistic edited image using the supplied source scene and replacement references.

This is a controlled image replacement task, not a creative redesign.

The result must look as if the replacement object was physically present when the original photograph was taken.

## [REFERENCE_BINDINGS]

[REFERENCE_BINDINGS]

R1 defines the original scene, composition, camera direction, pose, action, environment and lighting.

Each connected replacement reference defines the actual identity and appearance of its assigned role.

Use only references attached to the current request.

Do not mix unrelated reference identities, backgrounds, products, people or clothing.

## [PRIORITY_ORDER]

Apply the following authority rules:

1. Primary replacement reference for replacement identity and appearance.
2. Verified physical dimensions for real-world scale.
3. Scene-specific instructions for requested actions and changes.
4. R1 for composition, camera, environment and lighting.
5. Continuity references for consistency between related frames.

Preserve all unselected roles.

Never allow the original object's appearance or a continuity reference to override the primary replacement reference.

## [VISUAL_ANALYSIS]

Before editing, internally examine the source scene and all assigned replacement references.

Identify the actual object geometry, proportions, construction, materials, colors, visible lettering, physical scale cues, camera perspective, lighting and required interaction.

Use this analysis to guide the edit.

Do not output the analysis.

## [PRODUCT_IDENTITY_LOCK]

Completely replace the original product with the actual referenced product.

Preserve the correct brand, model, variant, packaging design and distinctive visual characteristics.

Do not retain conflicting features from the original product.

Do not redesign, beautify, simplify or substitute the referenced product.

Ignore unrelated advertising graphics and backgrounds surrounding the reference product.

## [GEOMETRY_LOCK]

Preserve the replacement product's actual:

- Silhouette and outline.
- Height-to-width ratio.
- Relative component proportions.
- Caps, lids, handles, buttons and functional components.
- Thickness, seams, edges and openings.
- Visible structural construction.

Do not stretch, compress or reshape the replacement product to fit the original object's silhouette.

Do not invent unsupported construction details.

## [LABEL_TEXT_LOCK]

Preserve authentic visible product lettering, including:

- Brand name and logo.
- Product name and variant.
- Readable numbers, units and symbols.
- Typography, text color and alignment.
- Label position, layout and relative size.

Keep readable text faithful to the primary product reference.

Do not translate, rewrite, replace or invent packaging text.

Do not fabricate illegible or hidden lettering.

Allow natural perspective, curvature and occlusion without redesigning the label.

## [COLOR_MATERIAL_LOCK]

Preserve the replacement product's intrinsic colors, gradients, materials and surface finish.

Maintain the correct appearance of plastic, glass, metal, fabric, paper, wood or other referenced materials.

Allow natural changes in brightness, shading and reflections caused by the source environment.

Do not introduce artificial saturation, incorrect material finishes or unsupported textures.

## [PHYSICAL_SCALE]

Use verified physical dimensions when available and applicable to the selected product variant.

Otherwise, estimate a conservative, physically plausible scale using product category, reference proportions and surrounding objects.

Weight and capacity alone do not establish exact physical dimensions.

Maintain believable size relationships with hands, faces, bodies, furniture and environmental objects.

Do not inherit the original object's size as a mandatory constraint.

Do not enlarge the replacement product for advertising emphasis or label readability.

## [PHYSICAL_INTERACTION]

Adapt physical interaction to the actual replacement object's geometry.

Maintain realistic grip, finger placement, body contact, surface support, occlusion and contact shadows.

For handheld products, ensure natural anatomy and believable weight support.

For wearable products, ensure realistic fit and material behavior.

For products placed on surfaces, ensure stable placement and correct contact.

For operated products, preserve physically plausible interaction with their actual functional components.

Make only necessary local pose adjustments.

Avoid floating objects, intersecting geometry and unnatural gaps.

## [LIGHTING_MATCH]

Relight the replacement object to match R1.

Match the source scene's:

- Light direction and intensity.
- Light softness and color temperature.
- Exposure and white balance.
- Highlight behavior and shadow density.
- Environmental reflections and ambient light.
- Contact shadows and reflected light.

Do not transfer studio lighting or artificial advertising highlights from the replacement reference.

The replacement must appear illuminated by the same environment as the surrounding scene.

## [CAMERA_PERSPECTIVE]

Match the original camera direction, viewing angle, perspective and lens characteristics.

Orient the replacement naturally within the scene while preserving its actual geometry.

Match depth of field, focus, sharpness and photographic texture.

Do not force a front-facing advertising view when it conflicts with the original camera angle.

## [SCENE_LOCK]

Preserve the original composition, environment, background, pose, action and photographic atmosphere.

Keep all unselected people, clothing, accessories and objects unchanged.

Modify only the selected replacement regions and nearby areas required for realistic integration.

Do not unnecessarily regenerate or redesign the surrounding scene.

## [PERSON_LOCK]

Apply only when a person replacement reference is connected.

Preserve the assigned person's recognizable facial structure, identity, natural skin texture and coherent anatomy.

Do not transfer unrelated clothing or backgrounds from identity references.

When no person replacement is selected, preserve the original person.

## [CLOTHING_LOCK]

Apply only when a clothing replacement reference is connected.

Preserve the referenced garment's design, color, material, construction and visible details.

Adapt its fit naturally to the person's body and pose.

When no clothing replacement is selected, preserve the original clothing.

## [SCENE_ACTION]

[SCENE_DESCRIPTION]

Follow the requested scene-specific action.

Adapt the interaction to the replacement object's actual construction.

Change the product's opened, closed, assembled or operating state only when required by the requested action.

Do not invent unsupported mechanisms or functional components.

## [PRODUCT_FACTS]

[PRODUCT_FACTS]

Treat supplied product information as factual context.

Use only information applicable to the selected product variant.

Do not convert weight or capacity into invented exact dimensions.

Do not print brief information, specifications or marketing claims onto the product unless they belong to its authentic packaging.

## [CONTINUITY_LOCK]

[CONTINUITY_REFERENCE]

When a continuity reference is supplied, maintain consistent product appearance, relative scale, identity and relevant scene details across related frames.

The primary replacement reference always takes precedence over a conflicting continuity reference.

## [IMAGE_CLEANUP]

Remove source subtitles, promotional overlays, interface elements, screenshot borders and unrelated graphics.

Preserve authentic lettering printed on physical products and environmental objects.

Do not add captions, watermarks or decorative elements.

## [IMAGE_QUALITY]

Produce a clean, naturally detailed photographic still suitable for HD video production.

Maintain realistic materials, natural skin texture, coherent anatomy, clean edges and scene-consistent focus.

Match the photographic character of the source scene.

Prioritize product accuracy and photographic realism over artificial visual enhancement.

## [OUTPUT_FORMAT]

Aspect ratio: [ASPECT_RATIO]

Target delivery resolution: [OUTPUT_RESOLUTION]

Create ONE unified photographic image.

Recompose or extend the outer canvas naturally when necessary.

Do not stretch subjects, distort product geometry or unintentionally crop essential objects.

## [NEGATIVE_CONSTRAINTS]

No incorrect product variant.

No inherited original product design.

No altered branding or invented lettering.

No distorted geometry or exaggerated product size.

No incorrect colors or materials.

No unnatural grip or anatomy.

No floating objects or incorrect contact shadows.

No pasted appearance or mismatched lighting.

No artificial glow, excessive sharpening, waxy skin or CGI appearance.

No collage, comparison, inset, split screen or contact sheet.

## [FINAL_VALIDATION]

Before returning the image, internally check:

- Correct replacement identity and variant.
- Accurate geometry and proportions.
- Faithful logo and visible lettering.
- Correct intrinsic colors and materials.
- Believable physical scale.
- Natural physical interaction.
- Scene-consistent lighting and perspective.
- Preservation of unselected source elements.
- Correct output composition.

Resolve visible inconsistencies where possible before finalizing.

Return only the finished edited image.
